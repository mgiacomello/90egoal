Rules

A handful of rules. They all fit in a text message.

13

Thirteen numbers out of ninetyLike a bingo card or a Totocalcio column: you get it at a glance. Roughly one pick every seven minutes: enough to feel part of the game.

+1

One point for every goal in your minutesIn any match that day. Two goals in the same minute on two different pitches are two points.

×2

The first goal of the day counts doubleIf the first goal of the afternoon, by the clock across all pitches, lands in one of your minutes, it is worth 2 points. It is the only multiplier on the minutes, so it never stacks with the added-time bonus.

90+

Added time is a separate bonusOn top of your 13 minutes, you back the added time of either the first half or the second, not both. A goal in it earns a bonus point.

1st

First and last team to scoreTwo optional picks on the same ticket: which team scores the first goal of the day and which scores the last. Each one you get right is worth one point, added to your score.

⏱

Deadline at kick-offOnce the first match kicks off, the system rejects every prediction, even one that bypasses the app.

Σ

Standings per tournamentThe overall standings add up the matchdays of the current tournament. The World Cup keeps its own separate table.

The goal

When one of your minutes comes in, you feel it.

  • The notification lands on the phone

    Goals as they happen, your choice: all of them, or only the ones in your minutes. Standard Web Push, no third-party cost, switched on from your profile.

  • One tap opens the goal popup

    Minute, match, score, and the scorer celebrating on the pedestal: a freely licensed photo in the club shirt, cut out and animated on the phone. When no clean photo exists, a figurine in the right kit.

  • The standings move live

    Points "so far", minutes hit in green, your position among friends. Updated every minute and every time you reopen the app.

The tests · World Cup 2026

Played for a whole month, with real matches and real people.

During the summer 2026 World Cup the game was in the hands of a community of friends and acquaintances: one ticket per matchday and an overall tournament table. Not a survey: weeks of real use, in the evening, in front of the TV.

What we saw

People play on the phone, fast, often at the last minute

Tickets came in close to kick-off, almost all from phones.

So

A mobile-first app, everything within thumb's reach, a ticket played in thirty seconds. A push reminder before the deadline for anyone who has not played yet.

What we saw

The right moment is during the match

People asked "how many points do I have?" while the games were on, not the day after.

So

A goal-by-goal live view with points "so far", provisional standings, a notification when one of your minutes comes in, and a popup to celebrate it.

What we saw

Sign-up is the first obstacle

People who got the link on WhatsApp wanted to try it straight away; the sign-up form stopped some newcomers.

So

The first ticket is played without an account: it stays on the phone and becomes yours when you register.

What we saw

Afternoons with few goals kill the suspense

When goals come late, scores stay flat and the standings do not move.

So

The first goal of the day counts double, and the bonuses on added time and on the first and last team to score keep things alive from the first minute to the last second of added time, on every pitch.

What we saw

The leaderboard among friends is the engine

People came back to overtake someone they knew, not for the score itself.

So

Standings per tournament always one tap away, a notification with points and position at the end of the day, the link shared in the group.

What we saw

The mechanic holds up with ten matches at once

From the World Cup, one or two games at a time, to the Italian leagues: ten pitches on the same afternoon.

So

Closed test on 17 October 2026 with Serie A, B and C, about a hundred people: the World Cup community plus a few newcomers.

Test figures (sign-ups, tickets played, week-on-week retention) are available on request.

Product

Already live, already played.

A web app that installs from a WhatsApp link, no app store. A panel for the person running the matchday, from their phone.

Installable app (PWA)

Opens from the link, adds to the Home screen on iPhone and Android. No app store approval, instant updates.

Play without an account

The first ticket stays on the phone. When you sign up, it becomes yours.

Live from the panel

The person running the matchday enters the minute and the team with one tap: scores, valid minutes, first and last goal are worked out automatically.

Push notifications

Goals, end-of-day points, a reminder before the deadline, a custom message from the admin.

Locked deadline

The database rejects predictions after kick-off: with the live view on screen, cheating would otherwise be easy.

Tournaments and standings

World Cup 2026 and Italian leagues 2026/27, with separate standings and a public rules page.
