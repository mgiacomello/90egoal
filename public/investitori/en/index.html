The game

Three gestures, the whole football afternoon.

No line-ups, no odds, nothing to study. Playing is faster than sending a message. Then every goal, in any match, becomes something that concerns you.

1

Pick 13 minutes

From 1' to 90', plus first- or second-half stoppage time. You pick them on your phone, in thirty seconds, even without signing up: your first card stays on the phone.

2

Watch the matches

Ten games from Serie A, B and C on the same afternoon. Every goal, on every pitch, falls in a minute. If it is one of yours, it is a point.

3

Celebrate the goals

The notification arrives: "Your 23' came in!". Tap, and the scorer celebrates on the pedestal. Live standings with your friends, from the first match to the tenth.

Rules

Few rules, all of them fit in a text message.

13

Thirteen minutes out of ninetyOne pick every seven minutes or so: enough to feel in the game, few enough to matter.

+1

One point for every goal in your minutesIn any match of the day. Two goals in the same minute on two different pitches are two points.

×2

The first and last goal of the day count doubleThe minute of the first and the last goal of the afternoon, by the clock across all pitches, is worth 2 points.

90+

Stoppage time is a separate pickChoose whether to back first-half or second-half stoppage time. Minutes count as the referee gives them.

⏱

Deadline at kick-offAfter the first match kicks off, the system refuses every prediction, even one sent around the app.

Σ

Standings per tournamentThe overall table adds up the matchdays of the current tournament. The World Cup stays available on its own.

The goal

When one of your minutes comes in, you feel it.

  • The notification reaches the phone

    Live goals, your choice: all of them, or only when one of your minutes comes in. Standard Web Push, no external cost, switched on from the profile.

  • One tap opens the goal popup

    Minute, match, score, and the scorer celebrating on the pedestal: a freely licensed photo in the club shirt, cut out and animated on the phone. When there is no clean photo, a figurine in the right kit.

  • The standings move live

    Points "so far", minutes hit in green, your position among friends. Updated every minute and every time the app is reopened.

The tests · World Cup 2026

Played for a whole month, with real matches and real people.

During the summer 2026 World Cup the game was in the hands of a community of friends and acquaintances: one card for each matchday and an overall tournament table. Not a survey: weeks of real use, in the evening, in front of the TV.

What we saw

People play on the phone, fast, often at the last minute

Cards came in close to kick-off, almost all from phones.

So

Mobile-only app, everything within thumb reach, a card played in thirty seconds. A push reminder before the deadline to whoever has not played yet.

What we saw

The right moment is during the match

People asked "how many points do I have?" while the games were on, not the day after.

So

Goal-by-goal live with points "so far", provisional standings, a notification when one of your minutes comes in and the popup that celebrates it.

What we saw

Sign-up is the first obstacle

Whoever got the link on WhatsApp wanted to try right away; the registration form stopped some of the newcomers.

So

The first card is played without an account: it stays on the phone and becomes yours when you register.

What we saw

Afternoons with few goals kill the suspense

When goals are late, scores stay flat and the table does not move.

So

The first and last goal of the day count double: there is something at stake from the first minute to the last second of stoppage time, on every pitch.

What we saw

The table among friends is the engine

People came back to overtake someone they knew, not for the score itself.

So

Standings per tournament always one tap away, a notification with points and position at the end of the day, the link shared to the group.

What we saw

The mechanic holds up with ten matches at once

From the World Cup, one or two games at a time, to the Italian leagues: ten pitches on the same afternoon.

So

Closed test on 17 October 2026 with Serie A, B and C, about a hundred people: the World Cup community plus a few newcomers.

Test figures (sign-ups, cards played, week-on-week return) are available on request.

Product

Already live, already played.

A web app that installs from a WhatsApp link, no store. A panel for whoever runs the matchday, from the phone.

Installable app (PWA)

Opens from the link, adds to the Home screen on iPhone and Android. No app to get approved, instant updates.

Play without an account

The first card stays on the phone. When you sign up, it becomes yours.

Live from the panel

Whoever follows the matchday enters minute and team with one tap: scores, valid minutes, first and last goal work themselves out.

Push notifications

Goals, end-of-day points, a reminder before the deadline, a free message from the admin.

Locked deadline

The database refuses predictions after kick-off: with the live view visible, you could otherwise cheat.

Tournaments and standings

World Cup 2026 and Italian leagues 2026/27, with separate standings and a public rules page.
